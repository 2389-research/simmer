# Iteration 2 Judgment

## Scores

## Per-criterion evidence

### atmospheric_density (9)
The two rooms flagged as thin in iteration 1 — Areas 5 and 7 — now layer multiple senses each. Area 5's stairwell adds temperature ("colder than the rooms on either side — breath hangs visible") and sound ("water drips into water at a steady, patient interval"), giving the formerly visual-only carpet/bannister description a body. Area 7 gains a thermal anomaly that does real Gothic work ("wrong-warm, blood-warm, the temperature of a body") plus a tactile pressure image ("silence has a weight... presses on the eardrum like deep water") — both are unusual sensory anchors that don't repeat what other rooms are doing. Crucially the additions don't dilute the existing strong rooms: Area 2's warm-wax chandelier, Area 3's nineteen-year-old steaming soup, Area 8's wet open page, and the closing bell-that-rings-once still anchor their scenes. Atmospheric uniformity is now near the ceiling. It stops short of 10 because the additions are still solid sentences rather than the genuinely uncanny set-pieces some rooms achieve (the corpses at the dining table, the husband-where-the-sun-would-be in the Orrery) — a 10 would require Areas 5/7 to carry an image that startling, not just be sensorily complete.

### dm_runnable_clarity (9)
The three concrete clarity gaps from iteration 1 are all closed cleanly. Abbreviated stat blocks for ghouls (AC 12, HP 22, claws +4 with DC 10 Con paralysis) and bat swarms (AC 12, HP 22, 4d4 bites, blindsight) appear inline at Areas 3 and 7 with tactics notes, so a DM never flips to MM mid-combat. The Widow's Awareness Track sidebar gives the chime mechanical teeth — round 1 listens, round 3 sends a scouting bat swarm, round 6 comes herself — and explicitly handles early-trigger conditions (reconsecration / coffin room / shutters) by skipping forward. The servants' tunnels are now seeded twice (Area 5 wall behind the descending stair, Area 7 behind the wine racks), with the Tactics paragraph updated to refer back to them rather than introducing them at the moment of use. Encounter list cross-references the inline blocks ("Stat block inline at Area 3"). The remaining 1-point gap to a 10: Veska's own stat block still sits at the back of the document rather than reproduced or thumbnail-summarized in the climax section, so a DM running the final fight either flips or pre-bookmarks; and the awareness track wisely allows "rounds or 10-minute exploration turns, DM's call" but doesn't give the DM a default — picking one would remove a small live-table decision.

### player_agency (9)
Agency was already at 9 and the iteration preserves every choice axis: six rumors, two routes, four-plus-one solution paths, three NPC-driven endings, and the Mira moral pivot. The new content actually strengthens agency at the margins — the seeded servants' tunnels mean a party that explored thoroughly can intercept Veska's escape (a previously invisible win condition is now reachable through play), and the awareness track means the party's pace and noise discipline now matter mechanically (a stealthy party gets more exploration time before she comes hunting; a loud party fights her sooner). Neither change introduces a dominant path. Holding at 9 rather than climbing to 10 because the core branching structure is unchanged from iteration 1 — this is consolidation of an already-strong agency design, not expansion of it.
