# Iteration 3 Judgment

## Scores
- atmospheric_density: 9
- dm_runnable_clarity: 10
- player_agency: 9

Composite: 9.33/10

## Per-criterion evidence

### atmospheric_density (9)
Atmosphere holds at the iteration-2 ceiling and gains exactly the uncanny set-pieces the prior ASI requested, without crowding the prose. Area 5 now carries the "child's handprint at adult shoulder height. It is fresh" — a single image that does more Gothic work than any number of additional sentences could: it tells the party Mira walked here recently, that something carried her, and that the manor remembers. Area 7 picks up "One closed coffin's lid has been opened and shut so many times that the wood is worn smooth in two places where a hand has gripped it" — again a single concrete image that doubles as an investigation tell (it's hers, no roll needed) and as a portrait of nineteen years of nightly ritual. The seed's strongest anchors all survive intact: the warm-wax chandelier, the steaming nineteen-year-old soup, the husband-where-the-sun-would-be, the wet open page in the Solar, and the bell that rings once and stops. It does not climb to 10 because the new images, while excellent, are still calibrated to match the existing top-tier rooms rather than to introduce a new register — the module's Gothic vocabulary is now fully even, but it is the same vocabulary throughout. A 10 would require something genuinely strange: a sensory beat that doesn't belong to the established palette (a sound the party knows shouldn't exist, a moment of dream-logic). The current artifact is uniformly excellent rather than occasionally astonishing.

### dm_runnable_clarity (10)
This is the criterion that moved decisively, and it has moved into clear-10 territory. The DM Combat Reference Card pulled to the front of the Climax section is exactly the artifact the prior ASI prescribed: Veska's key numbers (AC 15, HP 82, Multiattack with grapple-then-bite opener, Lullaby DC 13 Wis save, Regeneration's three shutoff conditions including the "hit by radiant/holy water last round" trigger, flee at HP 25 via tunnels in Areas 5/7) appear above the prose, and the four endings are rendered as an actual ASCII decision tree branching on coffin/day → solar → chapel → bargain. A DM running the final fight cold has every number and every branching choice in eye-line without page-flipping. The new "Timing Convention" section commits to 10-minute exploration turns with an explicit handoff to combat rounds at "comes herself" — the live-table structural decision flagged in iteration 2 is now removed. The awareness track sidebar is updated to reference the convention rather than contradict it. The flee clause in Tactics now refers back to the seeded tunnels rather than introducing them. Encounter list cross-references inline stat blocks. The decision tree's ASCII art is slightly cramped at the bargain branch (the "She drinks. Dies in his arms." text wraps awkwardly across two columns), but this is a typographic nit, not a clarity gap — the meaning is unambiguous. Every concrete clarity gap from iterations 1 and 2 is now closed, and the front-loaded reference card is the kind of thing experienced DMs actually use at the table. This is run-cold ready.

### player_agency (9)
Agency holds at 9, exactly as in iteration 2. The six rumors, two routes, four-plus-one solution paths, three NPC-driven endings, the Mira moral pivot, and the seeded servants' tunnels (which let a thorough party intercept Veska's escape) all carry forward unchanged. The decision tree in the new combat reference card is a presentation improvement, not an agency expansion — it makes the existing branches more legible to the DM but does not add new branches. The committed timing convention slightly tightens the consequence loop on party tempo (a stealthy party gets more turns; a loud one triggers her sooner), which is a marginal positive, but the change is at the level of pacing rather than choice topology. Holding at 9 rather than climbing because the core branching is the same shape it was in iteration 1 — three increasingly polished iterations have not (and arguably should not) added a new decision axis. The 1-point gap to 10 is the same as before: no genuinely emergent path that the module didn't pre-author. Two parties absolutely play different sessions; the design is textbook high-agency; pushing higher would require a different kind of move.

## ASI for next round
The remaining headroom is almost entirely in atmosphere — clarity is at ceiling, agency is at design ceiling for this artifact's scope, but atmosphere can still climb from "uniformly excellent" to "occasionally astonishing" by introducing one sensory beat that breaks the established Gothic palette. Pick a single moment — likely the climax arrival, or the chime in Area 2, or the moment Veska's Lullaby first lands — and write something that does not belong to the rest of the module's vocabulary: a sound the PCs recognize from their own past (a parent's voice, a battlefield they survived), a smell that shouldn't be there (sea air in the cellar, woodsmoke in the Solar), or a moment where the prose briefly drops into dream-logic and the boxed text describes something that cannot literally be true (the portraits all blink at once, the steaming soup spelling out a name as it cools). One such beat is enough; more would dilute. While there, tighten the ASCII decision tree's bargain branch typography so the wrapping doesn't suggest a column structure that isn't there — a simple linear bullet under "OTHER ROOM → Did Brennick bring the wine? → YES: She drinks, dies in his arms (Bargain Ending) / NO: Straight fight, expect 1-2 PC deaths" reads cleaner than the current diagonal text flow. These are the only two moves left at this scale; further iteration risks gold-plating an already-runnable module.
