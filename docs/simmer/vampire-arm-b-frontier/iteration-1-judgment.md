# Iteration 1 Judgment

## Scores
- atmospheric_density: 8
- dm_runnable_clarity: 7
- player_agency: 9

Composite: 8.0/10

## Per-criterion evidence

### atmospheric_density (8)
The boxed text is consistently doing real work — "the food is nineteen years old and yet only just cooling — steam still rises from the soup," the chandelier "dripping a thin black wax onto the marble. The wax is warm," and the bannister hounds with "eyes... gouged out" all give each room a distinct sensory anchor. The Lullaby reaction ("hums... the way someone hums who has forgotten what the song was for") and the closing bell that "rings once, and stops" land the Gothic tone with restraint rather than purple prose. Mood is unusually consistent room-to-room. It falls short of a 9/10 because a few rooms (Areas 5, 7) lean on a single image rather than layering smell + sound + temperature the way Areas 2, 3, and 8 do — the texture is excellent but not yet uniform.

### dm_runnable_clarity (7)
Strong scan structure: each area has a boxed read-aloud, a discrete mechanical hook, the DCs are pre-decided (DC 12 Persuasion, DC 14 Investigation, DC 15 Perception, DC 13 Religion), and the Veska stat block is fully written out at the back rather than hand-waved as "modified vampire spawn." The four-paths-to-kill-her summary in the Climax is a DM cheat-sheet in disguise. Where it slips below 8: the four ghouls and two bat swarms are referenced by MM page only (no inline stats), so a DM mid-combat still has to flip; the chime in Area 2 mentions "the Widow learns intruders are present" without telling the DM what she does with that information on which round; and the "servant tunnels" escape clause appears for the first time in the Tactics paragraph with a DC 14 Investigation that was never seeded earlier in the dungeon. Mostly clear, occasional page-flipping.

### player_agency (9)
Six rumors (one false), two routes in (causeway vs. cliffs that converge but gather different intel), four explicit ways to end Veska plus a fifth Ambrose-allied path, and three NPCs who each open or close a different ending (Brennick's poisoned wine, Ambrose's betrayal-alliance, Halma's cache and codex). The Mira subplot adds a moral axis orthogonal to the kill-vector axis. The "rewards investigation with safety" framing is delivered mechanically, not just promised — a party that gathers all six rumors really does enter pre-armed. Two parties would absolutely play noticeably different sessions; this is a textbook example of high-agency design.

## ASI for next round
Tighten DM-runnable clarity to match the agency and atmosphere ceiling — the module is one editing pass away from being runnable cold. Inline abbreviated stat blocks (HP, AC, attack line, save DC) for the four ghouls and the two bat swarms at the point of use in Encounters 1 and 4, so the DM never flips to the MM mid-combat. Add a short "Widow's Awareness Track" sidebar (one paragraph) that tells the DM what Veska does on rounds 1/3/6 after the chime — listens, sends bat swarm scout, comes herself — so the chime has mechanical teeth instead of vague menace. Seed the servant-tunnels escape route earlier (a single sentence in Areas 5 or 7 mentioning "a low servants' door behind the wine racks, DC 14 Investigation") so the Tactics flee-clause isn't a surprise. Finally, give Areas 5 and 7 one additional non-visual sensory anchor each (sound, smell, or temperature) to bring atmospheric uniformity up to the level of Areas 2, 3, and 8.
