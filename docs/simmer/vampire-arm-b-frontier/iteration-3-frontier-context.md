## Relevance to this iteration

iter-3 is the only frontier member — it strictly dominated iter-2 by lifting clarity from 9 to 10 while holding atmosphere and agency at 9. With no specialist alive on a dimension iter-3 lost on, there is no cross-member technique to import; the relevant comparison is internal.

The transferable technique within iter-3 is **the "single concrete image that doubles as an investigation tell"** — see Area 5's "child's handprint at adult shoulder height. It is fresh" and Area 7's "the wood is worn smooth in two places where a hand has gripped it." Each is one sentence that simultaneously (a) names a sensory anchor and (b) hands the party a no-roll inference (Mira was here, that coffin is hers). This is the move the candidate is *already best at within its current palette*, and it is the move worth extending — but the judge's ceiling note specifies that extending it within the existing register hits diminishing returns.

The next move is to apply the same compression principle (one sentence carries Gothic atmosphere AND a diegetic tell) but to a register *outside* the current palette. Specifically, the ASI names three viable shapes, and the choice is between them:

1. **Personal-history intrusion** — at the climax arrival or during Lullaby, a sound the PCs recognize from their own backstory (a parent's voice, a battlefield they survived). This breaks the third-person Gothic register by reaching into the players' established characters.
2. **Misplaced sensory** — a smell or sound that does not belong to this geography (sea air in the cellar despite the manor being on a tidal causeway, woodsmoke in the Solar with no fire). The wrongness is the tell.
3. **Dream-logic boxed text** — one moment where the prose describes something that cannot literally be true (the forty portraits all blink at once, the steaming nineteen-year-old soup briefly spelling out a name as it cools). This breaks the realistic frame entirely for one beat.

**Constraint:** insert exactly one such beat at one of three high-leverage moments (chime in Area 2, Lullaby first lands, or climax arrival). More than one would dilute — the judge is explicit that "one such beat is enough; more would dilute." The clarity-10 architecture (DM Combat Reference Card, Timing Convention, awareness track, ASCII decision tree) must not be disturbed; the new beat goes inside existing boxed text or as a one-paragraph addition to the climax-arrival passage, not as a new structural section.

**Secondary, trivial:** flatten the ASCII bargain-branch typography to a linear bullet — the judge flagged the column-wrap as a typographic nit, not a clarity gap. Do this in the same pass.
