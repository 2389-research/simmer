# The Widow of Hallowmere

*A self-contained Gothic vampire adventure for four PCs of 3rd level. Six paths in, four ways to end her, one rotting house in between.*

---

## One-Line Pitch

Lady Veska Halloran has been "in mourning" for nineteen years. The cattle agree; the village does not. Slay her — or strike a bargain that ends her quieter.

---

## Hook: The Contract

The party is approached in a roadside inn (or wherever the DM places them) by **Brother Onric**, a sweating, ink-stained cleric of Pelor. He carries a pouch of 200 gp, the seal of the village of **Hallowmere-on-the-Marsh**, and a list of seventeen names — the missing. He offers **300 gp on contract, 500 gp on proof of the Widow's death**, plus salvage rights to anything not nailed to the manor's bones. He will not enter the mansion himself. He says, plainly: *"My faith is not strong enough. Yours need only be louder than hers."*

If the party refuses gold, Onric offers a different lever: a child named **Mira Voss**, taken eight nights ago, may yet be alive in the cellars. Pick a hook; both work.

### Rumor Table (1d6 — five true, one false)

| d6 | Rumor | Truth |
|----|-------|-------|
| 1 | "The Widow only walks on moonless nights." | **TRUE.** She avoids her own reflection in moonlight; it pains her. |
| 2 | "Her husband still lives in the east wing, mad and chained." | **TRUE.** Lord Ambrose Halloran, her sire-thrall, is locked in the Orrery. He hates her. |
| 3 | "The chapel was deconsecrated when she rose. A priest of Pelor could undo it." | **TRUE.** Reconsecration requires the chapel's silver censer (Area 4) and 10 minutes of ritual. |
| 4 | "Sunlight cannot harm her — she is older than the sun's anger." | **FALSE.** Sunlight destroys her like any spawn. The shutters are nailed *from the outside*, by someone who knows. |
| 5 | "There is a society in the village that hunts her — and has failed twice." | **TRUE.** The Order of the Pale Lantern. They have a key, a stake, and bad nerves. |
| 6 | "She loved a mortal once. He still tends the gardens." | **TRUE.** Old Brennick, the groundskeeper, was her lover in life. He can be turned. |

A party that gathers all six rumors before approaching the manor enters with most of the puzzle pre-solved. **That is the design.** Investigation is rewarded directly with options.

---

## The Approach: Two Routes In

Hallowmere Manor sits on a tidal causeway. **The route the party picks shapes the first hour.**

- **The Causeway (front).** Walked openly. Brings the party past the **gardens** (Area 1) and to the **front foyer** (Area 2). Brennick may intercept them.
- **The Sea Cliffs (back).** A 60-ft DC 13 Athletics climb, or a found rope from an Order cache. Brings the party to the **chapel ruins** (Area 4) directly. Skips Brennick and the foyer entirely.

A party split between routes is *encouraged*. The two routes converge at the **central staircase** (Area 5), but the information they pick up is different — and the Widow's awareness of them is different.

---

## Timing Convention (read once, use throughout)

**Out of combat, count time in 10-minute exploration turns.** Each room visited, each search, each ritual prep is one turn unless stated otherwise. The Widow's Awareness Track (Area 2) ticks in turns.

**Once Veska "comes herself" — switch to rounds.** From that moment forward, count combat rounds normally until she falls or flees. The track does not tick further; she is already on the board.

This is the default. Override only if the table prefers otherwise.

## Mansion Map — Keyed Locations

### Area 1 — The Strangled Gardens

> *Hedges grown into one another like clasped hands. The roses are black at the edges, as if scorched, but they bloom. The smell is wet copper and lilac. A man in a wide hat is on his knees in the dirt, weeping without sound.*

**Brennick** (NPC below) is here. He is not hostile. He will speak if approached gently (DC 12 Persuasion / Insight to recognize his grief is for *her*, not for himself). He carries the **Garden Key** (opens Area 4 from the outside) and knows the location of the **journal** (50% chance it is here, in a tin box beneath the rosebush; otherwise it is in Area 6).

### Area 2 — The Foyer of Forty Portraits

> *Forty Halloran ancestors stare from the walls in oils gone black with age. Forty pairs of eyes track the door. The chandelier is iron, unlit, and dripping a thin black wax onto the marble. The wax is warm.*

A **DC 14 Investigation** check finds that one portrait — Lady Veska, age 23 — has been turned to face the wall. Turning it back triggers a faint chime audible throughout the manor (the Widow learns intruders are present; she does *not* know how many). **See the Widow's Awareness Track sidebar below for what she does next.**

A hidden alcove (DC 15 Perception) holds the **Order of the Pale Lantern's cache**: a +1 wooden stake, three vials of holy water, a sun-rod (functions as *daylight* once for 1 minute), and a note: *"Three of us went in. None came out. The next is you. — A friend."*

> **Sidebar — The Widow's Awareness Track**
>
> The chime is the trigger. **Count 10-minute exploration turns from the moment it rings** (default timing — see Timing Convention above).
> - **Turn 1 — Listens.** Veska sets down her book in the Solar and listens. No mechanical effect; the manor goes quieter (PCs feel watched: passive Perception 13+ notices the silence).
> - **Turn 3 — Scouts.** She sends one **bat swarm** (see Area 7 stat block) flying through the halls. It does not engage; it circles whatever room the party is in once and returns. PCs hear leathery wings overhead. If attacked, it flees — she now knows their numbers.
> - **Turn 6 — Comes herself.** She begins moving through the manor, *Spider Climb* on the ceiling, toward the party's last known position. Read the climax-arrival boxed text (page below) when she reaches them. **Switch to combat rounds from this point on.**
>
> If the party reconsecrates the chapel, enters her coffin room, or tears open the Solar shutters before turn 6, she comes early — skip to "comes herself."

### Area 3 — The Long Dining Hall

> *A table set for fourteen. The food is nineteen years old and yet only just cooling — steam still rises from the soup. Every chair is occupied by a corpse in fine clothes. None of them move. Yet.*

The corpses are **four ghouls** in stasis. They activate if a PC touches the food, sits in a chair, or speaks the Widow's name aloud. **Encounter 1 (combat).** A clever party can simply leave — nothing here is required.

> **Ghoul (×4) — abbreviated stat block (MM 148 for full)**
> AC 12 · HP 22 · Speed 30 ft. · darkvision 60 ft.
> **Bite.** +2 to hit, 1d6+1 piercing + 1d4 necrotic.
> **Claws.** +4 to hit, 2d4+2 slashing. On hit vs. non-elf, target makes **DC 10 Con save** or *paralyzed* 1 minute (save end of turn).
> *Tactics:* claws first to paralyze; bite paralyzed targets. They do not pursue beyond the dining hall doors — Veska's leash holds.

**Trade-off note:** This room is bypassed entirely on the cliff route.

### Area 4 — The Deconsecrated Chapel

> *The roof is gone. Salt wind moves through what was once a nave. The altar has been split with an axe; the halves face each other like teeth. A silver censer hangs from a chain that has not rusted, in a place where everything else has.*

The **silver censer** is the key to the *reconsecration* solution. Anyone proficient in Religion can perform a 10-minute ritual here that, once complete, makes the chapel **hallowed ground** for 24 hours — the Widow takes 10 radiant damage entering, cannot regenerate within, and is *frightened* while inside.

A statue of Pelor, beheaded, conceals (DC 13 Investigation) a brass key labeled *Orrery*.

### Area 5 — The Grand Staircase

> *The bannister is carved with hunting hounds. Each hound's eyes have been gouged out. The carpet is the color old blood becomes when it has been a long time becoming. The whole stairwell is colder than the rooms on either side — breath hangs visible — and somewhere below, water drips into water at a steady, patient interval. Halfway up the bannister, in the dust, there is a child's handprint at adult shoulder height. It is fresh.*

Both routes converge here. The staircase climbs to the upper wings; a smaller stair descends to the cellar.

A **DC 12 Perception** check reveals that the carpet is hiding the outline of a trapdoor (down to Area 7). A **DC 14 Investigation** check on the wall behind the descending stair finds **a low servants' door, half-hidden by a rotted tapestry** — it leads into narrow tunnels that connect the cellar (Area 7) to the upper wings without crossing the central halls. Veska uses these. So can the party.

### Area 6 — The Orrery (East Wing)

> *Brass planets hang on threads, slowly turning. A man is shackled in the center of the room, where the sun would be. He is thin, beautifully dressed in clothing nineteen years out of fashion, and his eyes are the wrong kind of bright.*

**Lord Ambrose Halloran**, the Widow's sire-thrall, is here. He is a **vampire spawn** but bound by silver shackles he himself forged before she turned him. He hates her. He will tell the party her three weaknesses (sun, stake-in-coffin, reconsecrated chapel) in exchange for a promise to end him afterward — or, if the party releases him, he will **fight beside them at the climax** (treat as an ally vampire spawn) but turn on the party once she is dead.

If Brennick's journal is not in the gardens, it is here, in a writing desk: *"She still loves him. She comes here every dawn and weeps. If she ever stopped weeping, I would know she was finally dead."*

### Area 7 — The Wine Cellar (Coffins)

> *The cellar smells of rot, vinegar, and something sweeter. Three coffins lie open. Three are closed. The closed ones are wet on the underside, as if recently used. The air down here is thick and warm — wrong-warm, blood-warm, the temperature of a body — and the silence has a weight to it that presses on the eardrum like deep water. One closed coffin's lid has been opened and shut so many times that the wood is worn smooth in two places where a hand has gripped it.*

Six coffins. **Only one** is the Widow's (the others are decoys; two contain her *bat swarms*, one contains a desiccated Order corpse with a half-driven stake, two are empty). A **DC 15 Investigation** check identifies hers (it has a single black rose on the lid; the others do not, but a clever decoy-maker also added roses to two others as recently as last week — careful examination shows the others' roses are fresh, hers is nineteen years old). A **DC 18 Religion** check identifies hers from grave-soil composition. *(The hand-worn lid is hers — a third tell, available without a roll to anyone who looks closely.)*

Behind the wine racks on the south wall, a **DC 14 Investigation** finds **a low servants' door** matching the one in Area 5 — the same tunnel network. (If the party already found the Area 5 door, they identify this end automatically.)

If staked in coffin during day: she dies, no fight. **Solution path.**

> **Bat Swarm (×2) — abbreviated stat block (MM 337 for full)**
> AC 12 · HP 22 · Speed 0 ft., fly 30 ft. · blindsight 60 ft. · resistant to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing
> **Bites.** +4 to hit, reach 0 ft., one creature in the swarm's space. *Hit:* 4d4 piercing (half if swarm is at or below half HP).
> *Tactics:* burst from disturbed coffins, screams alert Veska immediately (skip to "comes herself" on the awareness track), engage spellcasters in melee to disrupt concentration.

### Area 8 — The Solar (West Wing, Top)

> *A round room of windows. Every window is shuttered from the outside with iron nails as long as a finger. There is a single chair, facing east. There is a single book on a single table. The book is open. The page is wet.*

The Widow's lair. She is here at the climax if not found elsewhere. The shutters can be torn open from inside with a combined **DC 16 Strength** (two PCs assisting) — this floods the room with sunlight and is one of the four solution paths.

Mira Voss, the missing child, is alive in a wardrobe here, drained but breathing (one failed death save away). Saving her is the moral pivot for parties pursuing the bargain ending.

## Supporting NPCs

### Brennick, the Groundskeeper (mortal, age 67, ally-leaning)

A bent old man who was Veska's lover before she turned. He has stayed nineteen years tending her gardens because *he believes she still remembers him*. She does not. He knows this and continues anyway.

- Will reveal sun weakness for free.
- Will reveal coffin location for a promise of a clean burial for Veska.
- Will, if the party tells him plainly that she does not remember him (DC 14 Persuasion or honest play), break and offer the **mortal lover bargain**: he will go to her with poisoned wine they once shared, and she will drink it because she always drinks what he brings her. She dies quietly. He dies with her. **Solution path.**

### Lord Ambrose Halloran, the Bound Husband (vampire spawn, moral grey)

See Area 6. Allies of convenience. Will betray after victory unless the party puts him down. A party that frees him gains the **rival vampire alliance** path — he can fight her directly and has the stats to wound her significantly, but accepting his help is accepting a future problem. **Solution path.**

### Sister Halma of the Pale Lantern (mortal, ally)

If the party investigates the village (rumor 5), they find Sister Halma in the back of the apothecary. She is the only surviving member of the Order. She has the **second key** to the manor (back door, near chapel), three more vials of holy water, and the **Hallowmere Codex** — a 60-page hand-copied bestiary of vampire weaknesses. She will not enter the manor. She will pray.

### Mira Voss, the Missing Child (mortal, victim)

Eight years old. In the wardrobe in Area 8. Saving her costs a round of combat-time but is the only "innocent stake" the module presents. Parties who ignore her will be judged by Onric and the village. There is no mechanical penalty. There is a moral one.

## Encounters

### Encounter 1 — The Dining Hall Ghouls (combat, optional)

Four **ghouls** animate when a chair is disturbed. CR 1 each, total XP ~800 — a moderate encounter for level 3. Skippable on the cliff route or by careful play. **Stat block inline at Area 3.**

### Encounter 2 — The Garden Conversation (social)

Brennick. DC 12-14 social checks. Information for empathy. No combat unless the party draws steel — in which case Brennick simply lets them kill him, and the bargain solution closes.

### Encounter 3 — The Husband in Chains (social/combat hybrid)

Lord Ambrose. Pure roleplay until the party decides: kill him, free him, or leave him. Each choice opens or closes a different climax path.

### Encounter 4 — The Coffin Choice (exploration/puzzle)

Six coffins. One Widow. The party must decide: stake all six (uses all stakes/holy water, alerts the Widow via the bat swarms screaming), or investigate carefully. **Bat swarms** (2× CR 1/4) emerge from two of the coffins if disturbed. **Stat block inline at Area 7.**

### Encounter 5 — The Climax (combat/social/environmental)

See below.

## Climax: The Widow at Bay

> ### DM COMBAT REFERENCE CARD — read this first
>
> **VESKA (Widow of Hallowmere) — vampire spawn variant, CR 5**
> - **AC 15 · HP 82 · Speed 30 ft. / climb 30 ft. (Spider Climb)**
> - **Multiattack:** 2 attacks, only one a Bite. Standard opener: **Unarmed Strike to grapple (escape DC 13), then Bite the grappled target.**
> - **Unarmed Strike:** +6 to hit, 1d8+3 bludg., or grapple (DC 13).
> - **Bite** (vs. willing/grappled/incapacitated/restrained): +6 to hit, 1d6+3 pierc. + 2d6 necrotic; reduces target's HP max, heals her for the necrotic dealt.
> - **Lullaby (Reaction, Recharge 5-6):** target within 30 ft. that can hear her, **DC 13 Wis save** or *charmed* until end of its next turn. Open with this on the heaviest armor.
> - **Regeneration:** +10 hp at start of her turn unless in sunlight, running water, **or hit by radiant / holy water last round** (any of those switches it off for one turn).
> - **Saves:** Dex +6, Wis +3. **Resistances:** necrotic; nonmagical bludg/pierc/slash.
> - **Flee trigger:** at **HP 25 or below**, she breaks for the servants' tunnels (Area 5 wall / Area 7 wine racks) toward her coffin. A party watching either door cuts her off; otherwise she escapes — sequel.

The Widow has up to **four** ways to fall. The party engineers which one.

1. **Staked in coffin (Area 7) during daylight** — instant kill, no roll. Requires finding the right coffin during the day.
2. **Sunlight in the Solar (Area 8)** — tear open the shutters mid-fight. She takes 20 radiant damage at the start of each of her turns and is *incapacitated* on the first one. Effectively a one-round kill if pulled off.
3. **Reconsecrated chapel** — lure or drag her into the hallowed nave. She loses regeneration, takes 10 radiant on entry, and is *frightened*. A normal fight becomes a winnable one.
4. **Brennick's poisoned wine bargain** — Brennick walks into the Solar carrying a decanter. The Widow drinks. She dies in his arms. He dies hours later. The party witnesses; they do not strike a blow.

A fifth path emerges if the party freed Ambrose: he fights beside them, soaking her attacks. Treat as a 5-round fight where Ambrose buys two rounds before falling.

A direct frontal fight without engineering any of the above is *survivable but punishing* — expect 1-2 PC deaths. This is intentional. **The module rewards investigation with safety.**

### Setup: How the Climax Begins

If the party has not yet entered the Solar by nightfall, **she comes to them.** She finds them in whatever room they occupy when the chime tolls midnight (or when the awareness track reaches "comes herself"). Read aloud:

> *The candles all gutter at once. The temperature drops a degree, then five. From the hall, the soft tap of a single shoe on marble. Then another. She is humming — a lullaby, off-key, the way someone hums who has forgotten what the song was for.*

## Stat Block: Lady Veska Halloran, Widow of Hallowmere

*Adapted from vampire spawn. CR 5 — appropriate climax for level 3 PCs with engineered advantages, lethal without.*

**Medium undead (shapechanger), neutral evil**

- **AC** 15 (natural armor)
- **HP** 82 (11d8 + 33)
- **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

- **Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +3
- **Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
- **Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages** Common
- **CR** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Regeneration.** Veska regains 10 hp at the start of her turn if she has at least 1 hp and isn't in sunlight or running water. If she takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of her next turn.

**Spider Climb.** She can climb difficult surfaces, including ceilings, without an ability check.

**Vampire Weaknesses.** She has the following flaws:
- *Forbiddance.* She can't enter a residence without an invitation from one of the occupants. (Hallowmere Manor is hers.)
- *Harmed by Running Water.* She takes 20 acid damage if she ends her turn in running water.
- *Stake to the Heart.* She is destroyed if a piercing weapon made of wood is driven into her heart while she is incapacitated in her resting place.
- *Sunlight Hypersensitivity.* She takes 20 radiant damage when she starts her turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, she has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Veska makes two attacks, only one of which can be a bite.

**Unarmed Strike.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage. Instead of dealing damage, she can grapple the target (escape DC 13).

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing creature, or a creature that is grappled by her, incapacitated, or restrained. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and she regains hp equal to that amount.

### Reactions

**Lullaby (Recharge 5-6).** When a creature within 30 ft. that can hear her starts its turn, Veska may hum. The creature must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or be *charmed* until the end of its next turn — it sees her as a grieving woman, not a threat.

### Tactics

She opens with *Lullaby* on the most armored PC. She targets spellcasters with grapple-then-bite. She retreats to the Solar to feed and regenerate. **She does not fight to the death** — at 25 hp she attempts to flee to her coffin via the **servants' tunnels** seeded in Areas 5 and 7. A party that found and watched either door cuts off the escape; a party that ignored both lets her slip away, setting up a sequel.

## Denouement & Rewards

**If she dies:** Brother Onric pays out the 500 gp on proof (a fang, a fistful of grave-soil, Mira's testimony). The village holds a feast that no one quite enjoys. Brennick, if alive, walks into the sea at dawn.

**If Brennick's bargain ended her:** No body to display, but the village knows. Onric pays 300 gp ("contract fulfilled, not the proof I asked for"). Brennick is buried beside her, against village law, by Sister Halma, who breaks her vows to do it.

**If Ambrose ended her and lives:** The party has a CR 5 vampire spawn loose in the world and an unspoken debt. **Sequel hook.**

**Salvage.** The Hallowmere silverware (200 gp), the censer (50 gp, refuses to leave the chapel), the Hallowmere Codex (rare, 250 gp to a sage), the Order's +1 stake (keep), and Veska's wedding ring (100 gp; cursed; do not wear).

**XP.** 1,500 each for ending Veska by any means; +200 for saving Mira; +100 for promising Ambrose a clean death and keeping the promise.

**Final boxed text (if she falls):**

> *In the morning, the salt wind moves through the empty solar. The shutters are open. The book on the table has closed itself. Somewhere in the gardens, a bell that has not rung in nineteen years rings once, and stops.*
