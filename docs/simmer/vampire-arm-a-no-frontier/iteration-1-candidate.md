# The Hollow Heart of Ravenmoor

*A one-shot for four 3rd-level PCs. Estimated play time: 4-5 hours.*

**Pitch:** A widowed merchant hires the party to slay the thing wearing her late husband's face — a fledgling vampire haunting the family estate of Ravenmoor Manor.

---

## Hook: The Widow's Commission

Lady Iselda Voss meets the party in the back room of the Black Lantern Inn in the village of Ashgate. She is pale, thin, dressed in mourning silks two months past their season. She offers **300 gp now and 700 gp on proof of the deed** (a head, a stake-pierced heart, or the signet ring). She believes her husband Corwin Voss, dead three months, has risen as a vampire and now stalks the estate three miles up the valley road. Servants have fled. The last steward sent a letter, then nothing.

She gives the party:
- A hand-drawn map of Ravenmoor Manor (Areas 1-7 below).
- A silver locket containing Corwin's portrait (proof of identity).
- The estate's iron gate key.
- A flask of holy water (1d4+1 radiant, splash, save DC 13).

She will not enter the house. Press her and she admits: *"I think he loved me too well to truly let me go."*

### Rumor Table (1d6, roll once on arrival in Ashgate)

| d6 | Rumor | True? |
|----|-------|-------|
| 1 | "The Voss family kept a chapel in the manor — consecrated ground in the east wing." | True. Area 5. |
| 2 | "Corwin Voss was no saint. He had a mistress in the village — a girl named Tess who vanished a week before his death." | True. Tess is now a vampire spawn (Area 6). |
| 3 | "The wine cellar floods in the wet season. The whole foundation is rotten." | False, but the cellar is flooded ankle-deep (Area 7). |
| 4 | "I saw lights in the manor windows three nights past. Blue lights, like corpse-fire." | True. Will-o-wisp lure in Area 2. |
| 5 | "There's an old gardener, Hollis, who still tends the grounds. Mad as a sack of cats." | True. Hollis lives in the gatehouse and knows things (NPC below). |
| 6 | "The thing in the manor isn't Corwin. It's older. It rode him like a horse." | False — but the PC who hears it has advantage on Insight checks against the vampire's lies. |

## Approach to the Manor

The party can reach Ravenmoor three ways:

- **The valley road (default):** 1 hour walk. Pass the gatehouse (Area 1).
- **The orchard path (Stealth DC 12 to find, mentioned by Hollis or by examining the map):** Bypasses the gatehouse, enters via the kitchen garden directly into Area 4.
- **The cliff trail behind the manor (Survival DC 14):** Enters via the chapel window (Area 5). No alarms.

Each route changes which encounters trigger first. **The vampire is in Area 6 by default**, but if the party makes loud noise (combat, breaking doors) before nightfall, she relocates to Area 7 and prepares an ambush.

## Ravenmoor Manor — Keyed Locations

> **Time of day matters.** Daylight: vampire spawn are in coffins/dark places, take 20 radiant per turn in sunlight. After dusk: free-roaming, regenerating.

### Area 1 — The Gatehouse (exterior)

> *Wrought iron, rust-eaten. The gate sags. Beyond it, a gravel drive curves between dead yew hedges. A stone gatehouse leans against the wall, its single window lit by a tallow candle. Crows watch from the spikes.*

**Hollis the Gardener** lives here. He is not hostile but he is afraid.
- AC 11, HP 9, Str 8 Dex 10 Con 12 Int 9 Wis 14 Cha 8.
- Will trade information for a hot meal or 5 sp.
- **What he knows (give freely):** The chapel was deconsecrated by Corwin two months ago. Tess the mistress is in the cellar. The vampire sleeps in the master bedroom (Area 6) but wakes with the dark. The kitchen door is unlocked.
- **What he hides (Insight DC 13):** He was bitten three weeks ago. Hidden bite mark on his neck. He is a thrall — if combat begins inside the manor, he will lock the gate behind the party (DC 15 Strength to force).

### Area 2 — The Foyer

> *The doors swing open onto cold, still air that smells of wet ash. A grand staircase climbs into shadow. A chandelier hangs crooked, its candles gutted. Across the marble floor: muddy bootprints, dried, leading deeper in.*

- **Will-o-wisp** (CR 2) drifts down the staircase 1 round after entry. AC 19, HP 22, immune to most damage, **Shock** (+4 to hit, 2d8 lightning), **Invisibility** as bonus. It tries to lure a single PC up the stairs and into Area 3.
- **Tracking (Survival DC 12):** The bootprints lead to Area 4 (kitchen). A second set, barefoot and small, leads down (Area 7).
- **Investigation DC 14:** A portrait above the staircase shows Corwin Voss — but his eyes have been gouged out from inside the canvas.

### Area 3 — The Upper Hall (social/exploration)

> *A corridor of closed doors. The wallpaper, once green and gold, peels in long ribbons. At the far end, a music room: a harpsichord, lid open, one key sustaining a single note that will not stop.*

- **The harpsichord** plays a B-flat continuously. Investigation DC 13: a human finger bone is wedged under the key. Removing it stops the note. (Atmospheric, no mechanical effect — but if the players don't stop it, the vampire knows exactly where they are.)
- **Locked door to Area 6** (master bedroom). Thieves' tools DC 15, or the key is on the housekeeper's body in Area 4.
- **Window (west):** Overlooks the kitchen garden. PCs spotting the orchard path get a Survival check to map a retreat route.

### Area 4 — The Kitchen and Larder (combat)

> *The hearth is cold. A cleaver is buried in the cutting board. Strung from the rafters, where hams should hang, are three pale, drained bodies — the remaining staff. Their eyes are open.*

- **2 ghouls** (CR 1 each) crouch among the bodies, feeding. AC 12, HP 22 each, +2 to hit, 2d6+2 bite, **Claws (DC 10 Con save or paralyzed 1 minute, save end)**. They attack on sight.
- **The housekeeper's corpse** holds the master bedroom key (Area 6) and a pouch of 23 gp.
- **Larder (Investigation DC 10):** A trapdoor to Area 7 hidden under a flour sack.
- **Loot:** Silver carving set (worth 25 gp, **counts as silvered for one attack** if used as a weapon — improvised, 1d4).

### Area 5 — The Chapel (deconsecrated, exploration)

> *Smashed pews. The altar is overturned, the Saint's icon defaced with black wax. But sunlight, real sunlight, falls through the rose window in long colored bars across the floor — the only honest light in the house.*

- **Religion DC 13:** The chapel can be **reconsecrated** — 10 minutes of prayer + a holy symbol + clearing the wax. Once reconsecrated, **vampires take 10 radiant per round inside**, and a PC who fights here gains +1 to all attack rolls vs. undead. *This is the single biggest mechanical lever in the module.*
- **Investigation DC 12:** Behind the altar, a hidden compartment holds **Corwin's confession journal** (see "Three Solutions" below) and a vial of **holy oil** (coats one weapon; +1d6 radiant for 1 minute).
- **Rose window:** Can be smashed to flood the manor's east wing with daylight (DC 15 Strength or ranged attack vs. AC 13). **Major consequence:** any vampire within 30 feet of the resulting sunbeams takes 20 radiant/round in those squares.

### Area 6 — The Master Bedroom (climax — see below)

The vampire's primary lair. Detailed in the climax section.

### Area 7 — The Wine Cellar (combat / horror)

> *Ankle-deep black water. The smell of vinegar and copper. Casks line the walls; one has been smashed open and the wine has fed something. In the center, a stone sarcophagus — Voss family crypt, lid askew.*

- **Tess, vampire spawn** (CR 5, full stat block at climax — but she is here, not in Area 6, and she fights to defend the sarcophagus).
- **Trap:** Stepping within 5 feet of the sarcophagus triggers a **swarm of bats** (CR 1/4, AC 12, HP 22) bursting from the lid. Perception DC 14 to spot bat droppings on the lid first.
- **The sarcophagus** contains Corwin's empty coffin and a second, smaller coffin: Tess's resting place. Destroying the coffins (10 HP, AC 13) prevents her from regenerating overnight.

## Supporting NPCs

### Hollis the Gardener (thrall — moral grey)
*Old, bent, terrified, complicit.* Knows the layout, lies about his bite. If the bite is discovered, he weeps and begs for mercy: *"She said I'd see my wife again."* Killing him is morally fraught; sparing him risks betrayal. **DC 13 Wisdom (Medicine)** can determine he is 1-2 days from full turning.

### Lady Iselda Voss (patron — possibly compromised)
The widow. **Insight DC 16:** She knows more than she says. She *was* feeding her husband's hunger from a distance — sending servants up the road. Reveal this only if PCs return to confront her. She will pay the bounty regardless; her guilt is genuine.

### Corwin Voss (the vampire — antagonist)
Was a vain, philandering merchant in life. In undeath, he is wounded ego with fangs. He believes he still loves Iselda. He has taken Tess as both lover and lieutenant and resents both of them.

### Tess the Mistress (vampire spawn — antagonist, sympathetic)
Sixteen when she died. Furious, hungry, and entirely loyal to Corwin in a way that disgusts her. **If reduced to 10 HP or below**, she may surrender if offered a true death — Persuasion DC 15. This shortcut skips the Area 7 fight.

## Three Solutions to Corwin Voss

Corwin's confession journal (Area 5) names three ways to end him:

1. **Stake and behead in his coffin during daylight.** Coffin is in Area 6 under the bed. Auto-kill if surprised.
2. **Reconsecrate the chapel and lure him into it.** -1d6 radiant/round; PCs gain +1 attack. Lure: smash the rose window (sunbeams), or carry his coffin in.
3. **Straight combat.** Hardest. See climax.

Players who skip the journal can still discover (1) and (2) via Hollis (if pressed with Insight DC 14) or by exploring the chapel.

## Climax — The Master Bedroom (Area 6)

> *The room is preserved as if for a portrait sitting. A four-poster bed, curtains drawn. A vanity, its mirror covered in black cloth. Wedding portraits on every wall — Iselda, painted seven times. A music box on the dresser plays the same B-flat note as the harpsichord below. The window is shuttered and nailed.*

**Setup:**
- Corwin is in the bed (under the curtain) if it is daylight, **inside his coffin** (under the bed, requires DC 12 Investigation to spot the bed-skirt's lift-flap). At night, he stands at the vanity, looking into the cloth.
- **Props:** Music box (smashing it cancels the harpsichord-link; he loses ability to track PCs at distance). Wedding portraits (smashing them: he takes 1d6 psychic, DC 13 Wisdom save or **frightened** for 1 round — once per portrait, 7 portraits). Window shutters (DC 13 Strength to tear open; floods room with daylight if daytime).
- **Complications:**
  - If Tess still lives, she arrives from Area 7 in **1d4+2 rounds** of combat starting.
  - If Hollis still lives and is a thrall, he arrives in **1d6+3 rounds** wielding pruning shears (treat as scimitar, +3 to hit, 1d6+1 slashing).
  - The chandelier (Area 2) crashes if the floor is breached — atmospheric only unless a PC is below.

### Corwin Voss, Vampire Spawn (Elite)

*Adapted from MM vampire spawn, scaled for a level-3 climax.*

> Medium undead (shapechanger), neutral evil
> **Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
> **Hit Points** 65 (10d8 + 20)
> **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +3
**Skills** Perception +3, Stealth +6
**Damage Resistances** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks not silvered
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** Common
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP) — but with the chapel/sunlight/coffin levers in play, effective CR for prepared PCs is 3.

***Regeneration.*** Corwin regains 10 HP at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 HP and isn't in sunlight or running water. If he takes radiant damage or damage from holy water, this trait doesn't function at the start of his next turn.

***Spider Climb.*** Climbs difficult surfaces, including upside down, without an ability check.

***Vampire Weaknesses.*** He has the following flaws:
- *Forbiddance:* He can't enter a residence without an invitation from one of the occupants. (The manor is *his* residence — but the chapel, if reconsecrated, is not.)
- *Sunlight Hypersensitivity:* He takes 20 radiant damage when he starts his turn in sunlight. While in sunlight, he has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
- *Stake to the Heart:* If a piercing weapon made of wood is driven into his heart while he is incapacitated in his resting place (the coffin under the bed), he is paralyzed until removed.

**Actions**

***Multiattack.*** Two attacks, only one of which can be a bite.

***Claws.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 8 (2d4 + 3) slashing damage. Instead of dealing damage, Corwin can grapple the target (escape DC 13).

***Bite.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one willing creature, or a creature that is grappled by Corwin, incapacitated, or restrained. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken, and Corwin regains hit points equal to that amount.

***Charm (1/day, recharge after long rest).*** One humanoid Corwin can see within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 24 hours. The charmed target obeys verbal commands. Repeating the save each time it takes damage.

**Reactions**

***Mist Escape (1/day).*** When reduced to 0 HP outside his coffin, Corwin transforms into mist (instead of dying) and must reach his coffin within 2 hours or be destroyed. In mist form, he has resistance to nonmagical damage and can't take actions. **PCs who know this (journal, Religion DC 12) can station a guard at the coffin.**

**Tactics:** Round 1: Charm the most dangerous-looking PC. Round 2: Grapple-bite the charmed PC or a low-Con caster. Round 3+: Use Spider Climb to walls/ceiling, force ranged combat, retreat to coffin if below 20 HP. He talks the entire fight — pleads, threatens, offers "Iselda's mercy."

## Denouement

**On Corwin's true death:**
- His body crumbles to ash. The signet ring remains.
- The B-flat note in the manor stops. Ashgate's church bell (3 miles away) rings once on its own.
- If Hollis was spared and not yet fully turned, his bite mark fades within a week.
- If Iselda's complicity was discovered, she confesses freely and offers double the bounty for silence — or accepts arrest. Her fate is the table's.

### Rewards

- **Bounty:** 700 gp (1000 gp total with retainer).
- **Loot from manor:** ~150 gp in silverware, plus the holy oil vial, plus the silver carving set.
- **Magic item (DM's choice):** Corwin's signet ring is a *Ring of Mind Shielding* (he used it to hide his thoughts from Iselda in life — and from the senses of greater vampires in undeath).
- **Story rewards:** Hollis pledges service. The chapel, if reconsecrated, becomes a regional pilgrimage site. Tess, if granted true death, leaves a letter for her mother in the village.

### Continuing Hooks

- The journal mentions *"the one who made me — she rides north each spring."* A CR 13 vampire is out there, and she remembers the family name.
- Iselda, alive or dead, has a sister in a city three days' ride who will ask questions.
