# Iteration 1 Judgment

## Scores
- atmospheric_density: 7
- dm_runnable_clarity: 8
- player_agency: 8

Composite: 7.7/10

## Per-criterion evidence

### atmospheric_density (7)
The boxed text has real bite in places — "where hams should hang, are three pale, drained bodies — the remaining staff. Their eyes are open" (Area 4) and the harpsichord's single sustained B-flat note threading from the upper hall to the music box in the bedroom is a genuinely strong sensory motif. The chapel's "real sunlight... the only honest light in the house" lands. But several rooms thin out: Area 3's read-aloud is brief and generic (peeling wallpaper, closed doors), Area 6's setup is told mostly in mechanical bullets after the boxed text, and Area 1's gatehouse leans on stock Gothic furniture (rust, crows, yew) without a unique anchor. The dread is real but uneven — the prose peaks in 2/4/5/7 and goes administrative in 1/3/6.

### dm_runnable_clarity (8)
This is the candidate's strength. Stat blocks are inline at point of use (ghouls in Area 4, will-o-wisp in Area 2, full Corwin block in the climax), DCs are pre-set throughout (Investigation 12 for the bed-skirt, Strength 13 for shutters, Religion 13 for reconsecration), and the encounter summary table plus Three Solutions block let a DM scan the spine in under a minute. The Hollis stat line is compressed but sufficient. Minor friction: Tess's "full stat block at climax" is referenced but never actually printed (the climax block is only Corwin), so the Area 7 fight requires the DM to improvise or fall back to the MM. The vampire spawn weaknesses are listed cleanly, and tactics ("Round 1: Charm... Round 2: Grapple-bite") are spelled out. Strong, with one missing block.

### player_agency (8)
Three approach routes (valley road / orchard / cliff) genuinely change which encounters fire first, and the vampire relocates Area 6 → Area 7 based on player noise — that's real reactivity, not flavor. Three documented vampire solutions (stake-in-coffin, reconsecrated-chapel lure, straight combat) each have distinct mechanical scaffolding, and the chapel reconsecration plus rose-window-as-sunbeam is a meaningful, party-discoverable lever. Info-gating is layered: rumor table, Hollis (with hidden-thrall reveal), the journal, Iselda's complicity. Two parties would absolutely play differently. The dominant path bias is mild — daylight stake-in-coffin is clearly the strongest option and a savvy party will gravitate there — but the module openly acknowledges this and prices it (auto-kill *if surprised*; surprise requires they avoid noise). Tess's surrender option is a nice non-combat off-ramp.

## ASI for next round
The highest-leverage move is **rewriting the read-aloud and sensory anchors for Areas 1, 3, and 6 to match the density of Areas 4 and 5, and adding the missing Tess (vampire spawn) stat block inline in Area 7.** Each weak room needs one specific, unrepeatable sensory hook (a smell, a sound, a wrong detail) — for Area 6 in particular, the seven Iselda portraits and the music box deserve to live inside boxed text rather than be buried in the props bullet, because that room is the climax and currently reads as a checklist. Preserve everything that already works: the harpsichord motif, the Three Solutions structure, the route-and-relocation reactivity, the inline stat blocks and pre-set DCs, and the Hollis-thrall moral hook. Do not bloat — replace flat sentences with denser ones at equal length. The clarity score is hard-won; don't trade it for purple prose. Target: every room has one line a DM will remember a week later.
