## Per-criterion evidence

### atmospheric_density (8)
The rewrites in Areas 1, 3, and the Master Bedroom are a clear lift over the seed. The gatehouse now has specific, image-forward sensory anchors ("yew hedges... like fingers around a coin," wax pooled an inch deep, gravel that "smells faintly of cut onion") instead of the seed's serviceable-but-flat description. The Upper Hall's wallpaper "ribbons that curl... like tongues" plus the B-flat that "sits in the teeth" is genuinely transporting boxed text. The Master Bedroom is the strongest single passage in the module — the seven aging Iseldas, the "damp" silk hood, the "rosewater poured over old meat" — and the new hooded-mirror prop deepens the dread. Held back from a 9 because Areas 2, 4, 5, and 7 still read at seed-level density; the upgrade is uneven across the manor rather than systemic.

### dm_runnable_clarity (8)
Inlining Tess's full stat block at Area 7 is a real runnability win — no more flipping forward to "see climax" mid-cellar fight, and the surrender clause + tactics live next to the numbers. The encounter summary table, scan-friendly headers, and pre-set DCs all carry over from the seed intact. The hooded-mirror prop is added to the Master Bedroom but its 1d4 psychic trigger is well-bounded ("any PC who sees this and has known him in life"). Minor friction: the encounter summary still says "vampire spawn (CR 5)" for Tess and "see below" for Corwin, and the Tess stat block lives mid-document rather than alongside Corwin's, so a DM scanning by stat-block now has them in two places — net positive but slightly fragmented.

### player_agency (8)
Unchanged from seed at the structural level — three approach routes, three solutions, info-gating via journal/Hollis/chapel, and the Tess surrender shortcut all persist. The new hooded mirror adds a small atmospheric beat but no new decision branch. The seven smashable portraits remain seven discrete tactical choices in the climax, and the lever ecosystem (rose window, reconsecration, coffin staking, music box) is intact. No regression, no expansion — the architecture that earned the seed's 8 is fully preserved.

## ASI for next round
Push the atmospheric upgrade through to the rooms iteration 2 left untouched — Foyer (Area 2), Kitchen (Area 4), Chapel (Area 5), and Wine Cellar (Area 7). Each needs the same treatment the gatehouse and music room received: one specific, weird, non-generic sensory anchor that isn't a list of nouns ("smashed pews, overturned altar"). The chapel especially is load-bearing — it's the module's biggest mechanical lever, but its boxed text is the thinnest. Aim for one signature image per room (something like the cellar's wine-fed *something*, but realized) plus one olfactory or auditory detail. Preserve the master bedroom passage and Tess's inlined stat block exactly — those are wins. Do not add new mechanics; this is a prose pass.
